## Why MP4 is the right format for Playvids videos

MP4 (with H.264 video and AAC audio) is the closest thing to a universal video format:

- **Plays everywhere** — Windows Media Player, VLC, QuickTime, iPhone, Android,
  smart TVs, PlayStation, Xbox. No codec packs, no transcoding.
- **Streams well** — if you save videos to a home media server or NAS, MP4 is the
  format every server app expects.
- **Editable** — MP4 imports directly into CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci Resolve, and
  iMovie if you want to make clips.

Unless you have a specific reason to use another container, MP4 is the answer.
