- Update Chrome (or Firefox, Brave, Edge — any mainstream Android browser works).

## Step by step

1. **Open the video** in Chrome on Playvids and tap the address bar to **copy the URL**.
2. **Open a new tab** and go to [/](/).
3. **Paste the link** into the input box and tap **Download ↓**.
4. **Choose the quality** — 720p is the sweet spot on mobile data; 1080p if you're on
   Wi-Fi *(available when the downloader launches)*.
5. **Save the file.** Chrome shows a download notification; the MP4 lands in your
   Downloads folder *(available when the downloader launches)*.

## Finding your downloaded videos

Open the **Files** app (built into Android) → **Downloads**. From there you can:

- Play the video in any player (VLC is free and plays everything),
- Move it to your SD card if your phone supports one,
- Share it to another app — though remember, sharing copyrighted videos you don't own
  can get your accounts banned.

## Android safety notes

- **Never install a downloader APK from a random site.** Sideloaded "video downloader"
  apps are one of the most common Android malware carriers. A browser-based tool has
  nothing to install, which is exactly why it's safer.
- **Don't grant accessibility permissions** to any app that claims it needs them to
  download videos. That's never legitimate.
